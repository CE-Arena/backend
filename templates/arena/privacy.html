Your registration details

Registration
privacy.

The information you submit is used by CE ARENA organisers to review and manage tournament entries.

What the form collects

Registration asks for your gaming name and in-game player ID. Team entries also include the team name and the gaming names and player IDs of the other team members. CE ARENA records the tournament, submission time, registration status, and your acceptance of the competition rules.

The form does not collect game passwords, email addresses, or payment details.

How your information is used

Registration records are saved on the CE ARENA server so authorised organisers can review entries, check the submitted roster, and manage participation. Submitting a player ID does not verify the game account with a game publisher.

Who can see it

Public tournament pages do not list player IDs or team rosters. Your private registration link shows your gaming name, tournament, submission time, reference, and current status. Anyone holding that link can view those details. Keep the link private.

Registering a team

Submit a teammate’s gaming name and player ID only with their agreement, and check that each detail is correct before submitting.
